Research and coursework routes differ

Research degrees may be supported through university Research Training Program allocations and institution scholarships. Coursework awards are more often partial tuition discounts based on academic merit.

Read whether scholarship consideration is automatic with admission or requires a separate form, and confirm health-cover arrangements.

  • Separate research and coursework funding searches
  • Check scholarship closing rounds
  • Confirm supervisor requirements
  • Budget Overseas Student Health Cover
  • Read renewal and academic-progress conditions

How AspireCompass verifies listings

We summarise eligibility, funding and timing from the programme owner whenever an official page is available. Every listing shows the source and the date it was last checked.

Scholarship and recruitment details can change without notice. Always read the official call, confirm the deadline in your time zone and apply through the programme owner's portal. AspireCompass does not award scholarships or charge an application fee.
