How to use the Italy study cost calculator

Choose a city starting point, then replace every estimate with your own university, housing and travel figures. Enter tuition after any confirmed fee reduction and scholarship support only when its amount is documented.

The result separates recurring living costs, academic costs and arrival expenses. Use the funding-gap figure to decide whether the plan needs a cheaper city, different accommodation, a verified scholarship or more savings.

Why the first-month cash requirement matters

Annual affordability does not guarantee that the first month is manageable. Housing deposits, initial rent, travel, residence procedures and daily costs can arrive before any scholarship payment.

Keep a separate arrival target and verify when each scholarship instalment is expected. Do not rely on unconfirmed part-time employment to fund the visa or first weeks.

Replace estimates with primary evidence

Use the university tuition page, official scholarship call, written housing offer and current government instructions. Record the source and date beside every major figure.

Currency rates, utility costs and personal spending change. The calculator is a planning tool, not financial or visa advice.

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Are the city presets official prices?+

No. They are editable planning starting points. Replace them with current housing and living estimates for your exact city and situation.

Should I enter the full scholarship value?+

Enter only support that is confirmed and available for the same period. Consider payment timing and benefits delivered as meals or accommodation separately.

Does the calculator prove I meet visa financial requirements?+

No. Visa requirements are determined by the competent Italian authority and its current official checklist.

Does AspireCompass store my budget?+

No. The calculator runs in your browser and does not require an account.
