What fully funded should mean

The phrase normally indicates full tuition plus a living allowance, but benefits differ. Some awards add airfare, visa costs, health insurance, books or research support; others do not.

Read the current award conditions and estimate a realistic monthly budget for the destination before accepting an offer.

  • Tuition and mandatory university fees
  • Living or maintenance stipend
  • Travel and relocation support
  • Health insurance
  • Research, thesis or study allowance

How AspireCompass verifies listings

We summarise eligibility, funding and timing from the programme owner whenever an official page is available. Every listing shows the source and the date it was last checked.

Scholarship and recruitment details can change without notice. Always read the official call, confirm the deadline in your time zone and apply through the programme owner's portal. AspireCompass does not award scholarships or charge an application fee.

Compare coverage with a personal budget

For each award, copy the current benefit list into a budget and add every likely cost before the first payment. Mark tuition, stipend, travel, insurance, visa, tests, deposits, dependants and research support as covered, capped, conditional or self-funded.

Find the right award for your degree level

For bachelor's applicants, the University of Toronto Pearson award requires high-school nomination before its separate university and scholarship submissions. At master's level, Chevening, Erasmus Mundus and eligible DAAD EPOS courses use different course-selection and application systems. At doctoral level, Commonwealth and research-based university funding have their own nomination or supervisor requirements.

Funding labels also need context. The Hungarian government's Stipendium Hungaricum covers tuition and offers an allowance and accommodation support, but its official site warns the contribution may not meet full living costs. A careful comparison records which costs remain the applicant's responsibility.

  • Bachelor's: school nomination and undergraduate eligibility
  • Master's: course choice and admission sequence
  • PhD: supervisor, research scope and duration
  • All levels: net monthly budget after mandatory fees

Build a one-page funding comparison

For each shortlisted scholarship, enter the official programme, citizenship rule, degree level, application deadline, tuition support, stipend, housing, insurance, travel and conditions for renewal. Mark a benefit as 'not confirmed' when the current call is silent rather than filling it in from another year's announcement.

A candidate who needs full support for dependants or upfront visa and relocation expenses should calculate that separately; a scholarship called fully funded might still leave a large personal bill.

Italy: distinguish MAECI, DSU and university awards

Italy's government, regional and university routes solve different costs. MAECI uses an annual eligible-country call, DSU bodies assess regional income and merit conditions, and universities may offer a tuition waiver or merit award linked to admission.

The phrase fully funded should be used only after the current call confirms tuition, cash support, housing, meals, travel and insurance. The Italy scholarship guide records the current-cycle status and the official owner of each route.

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Does fully funded always mean I will pay nothing?+

No. An award can cover tuition and a stipend while excluding dependants, deposits, tests, visa costs or part of travel. Build a personal budget from the current official benefit list.

How should I compare two fully funded scholarships?+

Compare eligibility, degree fit, tuition, stipend, housing, insurance, travel, renewal conditions and costs that remain yours. Also compare the application route and decision timeline.

Can the scholarship and university have separate applications?+

Yes. Some awards have a separate scholarship portal or nomination stage, while others are tied directly to university admission. Follow the exact sequence stated by the programme owner.
