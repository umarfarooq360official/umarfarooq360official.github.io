Lester B. Pearson Scholarship: nomination to application

A student cannot start this scholarship with a stand-alone funding form. Their high school nominates them, then they apply for University of Toronto admission and complete a separate award application.

Updated 20 Sept 2026 Global English Editorial guide

Use this guide with the official instructions.

Programme prompts, limits and document rules always take priority over general advice.

Three different dates for September 2027 entry

The University of Toronto lists 9 October 2026 for school nomination, 16 October 2026 for the student's undergraduate admission application, and 6 November 2026 for the Pearson scholarship application and documents. Missing the first deadline cannot be fixed simply by completing the later form.

Have your school's counsellor or head confirm its participation early. Read the university's current scholarship page again immediately before acting, since universities can change application instructions.

  • 9 October 2026: school nomination
  • 16 October 2026: University of Toronto admission application
  • 6 November 2026: scholarship form and required documentation

Who is eligible for consideration?

The official 2027 entry guidance targets international students who need a Canadian study permit and are in their final year of secondary school in 2026/27 or graduated no earlier than June 2026. Students already in post-secondary education and those beginning post-secondary education in January 2027 are not eligible under the current guidance.

Nomination is competitive: a high school can nominate one student each year. Strong academics alone are not the whole selection story; the university describes creativity, leadership and contribution to the school or community as important qualities.

How the school nomination works

Ask a guidance counsellor, principal or school head whether your school is authorised to nominate. A school not already participating must take the official registration route; individual students cannot replace the school's nomination with an agent or a private message.

Provide your school with a short evidence file: academic record, substantive projects, leadership contributions and a clear explanation of why University of Toronto study fits your goals. The school makes its own nomination decision.

Apply to the right University of Toronto programme

After nomination, apply to at least one eligible undergraduate programme through the university's stated admission route before its Pearson consideration deadline. The university says an eventual scholarship would be tied to the student's top choice of programme at the time of the award decision.

Check admission prerequisites and English-language evidence for that exact programme separately. Scholarship nomination does not guarantee admission, a study permit or an award.

Complete the scholarship form yourself

The university sends nominated students a private scholarship application link after nomination. Complete the form using your own genuine experiences and the university's current rules. The university explicitly prohibits generative AI tools from creating content for its undergraduate award application; applicants should follow that rule.

A scholarship website can explain the process, but it must not draft a nominee's restricted responses. Treat offers from agents claiming a guaranteed nomination or scholarship as suspicious; the university warns that the Pearson programme does not recruit through educational agents.

Check the real funding package

The official page lists tuition, books, incidental fees and full residence support for four years. Ask the university about anything the published award description does not specify, such as travel, visa, personal expenses or how later-year support is maintained.

Keep a realistic budget and an alternative funding shortlist. Even a strong nominee should not treat admission or an award as certain before receiving the university's official decision.

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Can I apply without a school nomination?+

No. The University of Toronto requires a nomination from the student's high school before the individual scholarship application is provided.

Can my school nominate more than one student?+

The university's current guidance says one student per school per year.

Does the award pay for all travel and visa costs?+

The official scholarship page lists tuition, books, incidental fees and full residence support. Confirm any other expenses directly with the programme office.
