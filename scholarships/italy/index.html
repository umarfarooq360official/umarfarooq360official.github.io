Scholarship status must include an academic year

A scholarship name can remain online after its deadline. Always pair the name with the academic year, application owner and official closing time. On 29 September 2026, the MAECI and main ER.GO 2026–2027 competitions shown here are closed; future calls require new dates from their owners.

University admission may open before a scholarship notice is published. Do not describe a future university award as open because the degree portal accepts applications.

MAECI Italian Government grants

MAECI publishes an annual call for foreign citizens and Italians living abroad, an eligible-country list and instructions for the Study in Italy portal. The 2026–2027 call closed at 14:00 Italian time on 26 March 2026 and covered study carried out in Italy.

Before a future application, verify that your country, course type, age, academic background and language evidence meet the new call. Also confirm the grant duration, health-insurance terms, tuition treatment and any costs that remain yours.

Regional DSU scholarships

DSU is a right-to-study system administered through regional or institution-linked bodies. Names, portals, deadlines, benefit amounts and document rules vary. ER.GO serves institutions in Emilia-Romagna; other regions use different bodies and calls.

Applicants whose family income or assets are outside Italy commonly need official financial and family documents, translations and legalisation or authentication that meet the relevant call. Start these documents early because admission documents alone are not enough for an economic assessment.

University merit scholarships and fee waivers

The University of Padua's 2026–2027 International Excellence award advertised €8,000 gross per academic year plus a tuition-fee exemption for selected English-taught bachelor's and master's students, with the regional fee and revenue stamp still payable. The award was normally considered through the degree application when the published conditions were met.

Politecnico di Milano's 2026–2027 international merit route offered full tuition waivers and, for some awards, a gross allowance up to €10,000 a year. Its published conditions tied eligibility to the early admission period and a separate scholarship form. These facts explain the route; a later intake needs its own call.

Compare funding using the same fields

Write down tuition coverage, mandatory charges, cash stipend, accommodation, meals, travel, insurance, duration, payment schedule and renewal conditions. A fee waiver and a living-cost scholarship solve different parts of the budget.

Calculate how much cash you need before arrival and before the first payment. Housing deposits, visa-related costs and initial travel can be due earlier than scholarship disbursement.

  • Academic year and current status
  • Eligible nationality and degree level
  • Separate admission required
  • Income or merit assessment
  • Covered and excluded costs
  • Renewal and credit requirements

Build a safe application sequence

Open the official call, confirm eligibility and save the dated document. Apply through the named government, regional or university portal. Record every dependent action, including admission, financial documentation, language evidence and pre-enrolment.

Avoid anyone promising a guaranteed DSU, MAECI or university award. Never send passwords or pay a private account to secure selection. Use the contacts published on the official domain when a rule is unclear.

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Is the Italy DSU scholarship fully funded?+

Benefits can include money and services such as accommodation or meals, but the package and amount depend on the regional call, student status and assessment. Read the exact current call before using the label fully funded.

Can I apply for MAECI and a university scholarship together?+

Check both calls for incompatibility rules. Some awards cannot be combined, and admission may remain a separate requirement.

Does admission automatically apply me for every university scholarship?+

No. Some merit awards use automatic consideration, while others require a separate form or financial documents. Follow the named award's procedure.

Can Pakistani students apply for scholarships in Italy?+

They can apply to routes whose current nationality, degree, academic, financial and language conditions they meet. Confirm Pakistan on any eligible-country list and read the current call.
