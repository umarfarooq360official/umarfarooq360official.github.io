## Ongoing publishing sequence

1. Update open/closing records weekly and keep `dateChecked` accurate.
2. Expand one country cluster at a time with genuinely useful local requirements.
3. Add programme detail pages only after collecting official eligibility, funding, deadline and application-source data.
4. Link every new article to one parent category, one relevant country and two closely related guides.
5. Avoid publishing thin or copied deadline posts; one maintained evergreen page is stronger than several stale duplicates.
