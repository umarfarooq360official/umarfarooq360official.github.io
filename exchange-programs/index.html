Ask these questions before applying

Exchange programmes often involve both a home and host institution. A nomination deadline can occur weeks before the public deadline, so contact your international office early.

  • Is home-university nomination required?
  • Will credits transfer to your degree?
  • Which tuition and living costs are covered?
  • Are language courses included?
  • What visa and insurance documents are needed?

How AspireCompass verifies listings

We summarise eligibility, funding and timing from the programme owner whenever an official page is available. Every listing shows the source and the date it was last checked.

Scholarship and recruitment details can change without notice. Always read the official call, confirm the deadline in your time zone and apply through the programme owner's portal. AspireCompass does not award scholarships or charge an application fee.

Protect your degree progress

Ask your academic department to confirm in writing how proposed host courses will transfer. Course availability can change, so prepare alternatives that still meet your home-degree requirements.

Check the academic calendar, assessment dates and housing period before booking travel. An exchange grant may arrive after departure, which means you may need a temporary cash plan even when funding is approved.

Budget beyond the programme grant

Compare flights, visa or residence fees, insurance, housing deposits, local transport and exchange-rate risk. Ask whether tuition is waived at the host institution and which fees remain payable at home.

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Can I apply for more than one opportunity?+

Usually yes, unless a programme's official rules restrict parallel applications. Keep a tracker for deadlines, references and programme-specific documents.

Does AspireCompass accept applications?+

No. AspireCompass helps you discover and compare opportunities. Applications must be submitted on the official programme or institution website.

Does AspireCompass submit applications for students?+

No. AspireCompass is an independent research and guidance website. Apply only through the programme, university, government or employer portal linked as the official source.

Can a deadline or eligibility rule change after this page is updated?+

Yes. Programme owners can revise dates, benefits and rules. Reopen the official call before you act, check the intake year and save a copy of the instructions used for your application.
