One story, adapted carefully

Your CV, essays, references and interview should reinforce the same core direction while answering different questions. Reusing facts is fine; copying identical paragraphs into every application is not.

  • Create a master evidence bank
  • Map every prompt to one clear example
  • Use outcomes and reflection
  • Remove unsupported claims
  • Proofread names, dates and programme details

Choose the guide that solves the next bottleneck

Start with eligibility and process before improving prose. If the shortlist is sound but the story is unclear, work on the evidence bank and statement of purpose. If the written application is submitted, move to interview preparation rather than repeatedly rewriting old documents.

Every guide is general educational support. The current programme prompt, word limit, document format and permitted use of AI always take priority.

Use AI without losing accuracy or authorship

Check each programme's rules. If assistance is allowed, use tools to organise ideas, find gaps or improve clarity, then verify every fact and rebuild the final response in your own voice. Never invent achievements, references, research or personal experience.

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Can I copy an AspireCompass example into my application?+

No. Examples demonstrate structure only. Selection committees need your own evidence and voice, and copied wording can violate academic or application rules.

Which application document should I prepare first?+

Begin with a master evidence bank and accurate CV. These make it easier to draft programme-specific essays, brief referees and prepare interview examples.

Does AspireCompass submit applications for students?+

No. AspireCompass is an independent research and guidance website. Apply only through the programme, university, government or employer portal linked as the official source.

Can a deadline or eligibility rule change after this page is updated?+

Yes. Programme owners can revise dates, benefits and rules. Reopen the official call before you act, check the intake year and save a copy of the instructions used for your application.
