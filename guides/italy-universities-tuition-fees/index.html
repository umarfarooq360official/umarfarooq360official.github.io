How to read an Italian tuition figure

A single number rarely describes every student. The payable amount can depend on the programme, ECTS load, family economic indicator, foreign-income documentation, citizenship or residence category and any exemption. Application fees, regional tax, stamp duty and insurance can sit outside an advertised tuition waiver.

The figures below come from official pages for 2026–2027 and provide a comparison method. Confirm the exact programme and your assessed amount before accepting an offer.

University of Padua

Padua states that enrolling in its degree programmes costs from zero to approximately €3,000 per year. It also publishes a no-tax area, concessions and full or partial fee waivers for eligible students. The final result depends on the 2026–2027 fee and exemption notice and the student's circumstances.

International funding routes include the Padua International Excellence Scholarship, 100 tuition fee waivers for students in eligible English-taught programmes under the published scheme, departmental awards and regional support. Check whether consideration is automatic with admission or requires another action for the intake you select.

  • Published general range: €0 to about €3,000 per year
  • Check regional fee and revenue stamp
  • Request concessions by the stated deadline
  • Match the scholarship to an English-taught degree and current intake

Politecnico di Milano

For a standard 46–74 ECTS study plan, Polimi publishes an annual amount from €157.04 up to €3,943.04 according to economic and study-plan conditions. Its 2026–2027 page states a first enrolment instalment of €880.04, with possible reduction through the stated ISEE process, and a later second instalment based on the relevant assessment.

Reserved non-EU master's students admitted with a foreign first degree who do not receive a qualifying scholarship are charged the maximum according to the study-plan percentage under the published rule. Merit scholarships can provide a full tuition waiver and some include an allowance, while DSU follows a separate economic and merit process.

  • Published standard annual range: €157.04 to €3,943.04
  • 2026–2027 first instalment stated as €880.04 before an applicable reduction
  • Foreign-income students may need Equalised ISEEU
  • Check merit and DSU applications separately

University of Bologna

Bologna describes its fee as a fixed €157.04 instalment plus a variable contribution based on the degree programme and the family's economic situation. The maximum varies by programme. Applicants who do not submit the required ISEE or foreign-income documents can be charged the full programme amount.

For 2026–2027, the university listed the first instalment by 24 September 2026, followed by possible instalments on 18 December 2026 and 15 March 2027. A degree can set a different first deadline. ER.GO scholarship and accommodation applications use their own calendar.

  • Fixed component: €157.04
  • Variable amount: programme and economic assessment
  • Foreign-income documents affect reductions
  • ER.GO and university fee deadlines must both be tracked

Compare the net annual cost

For each university, start with the maximum amount applicable to your category. Subtract only a confirmed waiver or award, then add mandatory charges, housing, food, transport, insurance, residence-permit costs, travel and study materials. Keep the first-year arrival budget separate from the later annual budget.

A lower tuition figure can be outweighed by a higher housing cost, while a large scholarship can arrive after deposits are due. Compare the timing of cash as well as the annual total.

A university comparison worksheet

Record one answer and one official URL for every field. A blank answer is a research task, not permission to assume zero.

  • Degree and teaching language
  • Admission and application fee
  • Maximum tuition for your category
  • Economic-document deadline
  • Mandatory regional and administrative charges
  • Merit scholarship
  • Regional DSU route
  • Estimated monthly housing
  • First payment date and scholarship payment date

Avoid misleading fee comparisons

Do not compare one university's minimum subsidised figure with another university's maximum standard fee. Use the same applicant profile and study load. Distinguish a tuition waiver from a cash scholarship and label every figure with its academic year.

Before paying, reopen the official fee notice from the university account or degree page. A third-party article cannot calculate your final bill.

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Which Italian university is cheapest for international students?+

There is no reliable answer without the programme and applicant profile. Compare the amount applicable to your category after economic assessment, then include mandatory charges and city living costs.

Will I pay the minimum fee if my family income is low?+

Only if you meet the university's conditions and submit accepted economic documents by its deadline. Foreign income and assets may require a specific equivalent assessment.

Does a full tuition waiver cover living costs?+

No. A tuition waiver removes eligible tuition but normally does not pay housing, food, travel or every mandatory charge unless the award explicitly says so.

Are application fees included in annual tuition?+

Often they are separate and may be non-refundable. Check the admission page as well as the tuition page.
