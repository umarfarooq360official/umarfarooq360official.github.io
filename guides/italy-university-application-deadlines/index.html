Italy university application deadlines for 2027 entry

Use this dated tracker to identify the application window for your exact university, degree and applicant category. Italy does not have one national admission deadline, so every date must remain attached to its programme and official source.

Quick answer: applications are already opening for 2027–2028

The University of Padua opened its first call for most 2027–2028 programmes on 15 September 2026, with the first window closing on 15 November 2026. Programme exceptions and later calls must be checked on the individual course page.

Politecnico di Milano lists separate 2027–2028 windows by study area. Its published master's timetable includes an early period beginning 1 October 2026, while later windows and eligibility differ by programme and applicant status.

  • Padua first call: 15 September–15 November 2026 for most listed programmes
  • Polimi early window: begins 1 October 2026 for listed 2027–2028 master's routes
  • Bologna and other universities: verify the exact programme call
  • Scholarship deadlines may be earlier or separate

Do not use a university-wide date without the programme call

Architecture, engineering, design, medicine and limited-enrolment courses can follow different calendars. The date for one English-taught master's degree may not apply to another department at the same university.

Record the degree title, level, campus, applicant category, application portal, opening date, closing time and time zone. Save the official call or programme page used for the entry.

A practical 2027 application calendar

From September to December 2026, prioritise programmes with early calls, language evidence and scholarship-linked admission windows. From January to March 2027, complete later calls and prepare Universitaly and visa documents after admission.

Non-EU applicants living outside Italy should avoid relying on late rounds that may be limited to EU applicants or people already resident in Italy. Earlier submission also leaves more time for qualification recognition and visa preparation.

  • September–October 2026: shortlist and prepare documents
  • October–December 2026: submit early university and scholarship applications
  • January–March 2027: use eligible later calls
  • After admission: complete Universitaly pre-enrolment
  • Before travel: finish visa and accommodation planning

Separate four types of deadline

A strong tracker has separate rows for university admission, scholarship or fee support, Universitaly pre-enrolment and the visa. Combining them into one date creates missed steps.

Also record document deadlines. A language certificate, portfolio, test, CIMEA statement or financial record may be due before final enrolment even when the initial application accepts a temporary document.

  • Admission application
  • Application fee payment
  • Language or test evidence
  • Scholarship application
  • Universitaly pre-enrolment
  • Visa submission
  • Final enrolment and original documents

How to verify a deadline in five minutes

Open the official programme page, locate the current academic year and identify your applicant category. Confirm whether the displayed time uses CET or CEST, then check whether payment and document upload must also finish before the same cut-off.

Recheck the page one week before submission and again on the day you apply. Keep the confirmation email and a copy of the submitted file.

Weekly tracker template

Review the tracker once a week. Mark each record as researching, preparing, submitted, decision received or closed. Add the last-checked date and the official URL instead of relying on a remembered date.

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

What is the Italy university application deadline for 2027?+

There is no single national deadline. Each university and programme publishes its own calls, and applicant category can change which round is available.

When did Padua applications open for 2027–2028?+

The University of Padua states that the first call for most programmes opened on 15 September 2026 and closes on 15 November 2026. Check the exact programme for exceptions.

Can non-EU students apply in the last call?+

Not always. Some later calls are limited to EU applicants or non-EU applicants already resident in Italy, so verify the category written in the official call.

Is the scholarship deadline the same as admission?+

Often it is separate and can be earlier. Track admission and every funding application independently.
