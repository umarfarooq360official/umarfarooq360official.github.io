Study in Italy: admission and English-language requirements

Build an Italy application in the correct order and verify English evidence at programme level. This guide explains where IELTS, another certificate or prior English-medium education may fit.

Quick answer: there is no single Italy IELTS rule

The university and exact degree determine the academic and language requirement. A programme may require B1 or B2 English, list accepted test scores, permit documented prior education in English or set an internal assessment. Another programme at the same university can use a different rule.

Use three checks: the degree call, the university's accepted-language-evidence page and the deadline for uploading proof. A scholarship's silence about IELTS does not cancel the university requirement.

University of Padua English evidence

For 2026–2027, Padua states that English-taught programmes and some Italian-taught programmes require the level named in the individual admission notice, commonly B1 or B2. The university publishes accepted certificates and minimum scores for the relevant language level.

Padua also describes an alternative based on a completed bachelor's, single-cycle master's or master's qualification taught entirely in English. The submitted records must explicitly show the language of instruction; otherwise, an official university document confirming it is required. The programme call remains controlling.

Politecnico di Milano master's English evidence

Polimi's published master's requirements list IELTS Academic or General Training at 6 or above, TOEFL iBT at 78 or above, PTE Academic at 59 or above and other named certificates under their specific conditions. Applicants must upload accepted proof by the stated admission deadline.

International students admitted to English-language master's programmes also receive an Italian-language obligation that must be satisfied before graduation under the university's current rule. This later Italian requirement should not be confused with the English admission requirement.

When a Medium of Instruction document may help

An MOI letter can help only where the institution permits prior English-medium education as an alternative. It should be issued by the awarding institution and state that the complete relevant qualification was taught in English. Studying a few courses in English may not satisfy a full-qualification rule.

Send the exact programme and intake to admissions when the policy is unclear. Keep the written answer and still meet any deadline for uploading evidence. Do not rely on a social-media claim that every Italian university accepts MOI.

Bachelor's and master's academic admission

A bachelor's or single-cycle application with a foreign qualification normally requires a school history equivalent to at least 12 years, subject prerequisites and any course-specific test or ranking. Applicants from shorter school systems should read the university's permitted compensating study or foundation routes.

A master's application is assessed against the prior degree, subjects, credits, academic performance and any portfolio or programme-specific evidence. Polimi tells foreign-degree applicants to check country-specific qualification and GPA requirements before submitting its master's application.

Application documents to prepare

Use the selected programme's checklist. Common items include passport, qualification or current enrolment evidence, transcript, grading information, language proof, translations and programme-specific statements or portfolio. A positive initial evaluation can still require original, verified or recognition documents for enrolment.

Names and dates should match the passport. Upload complete scans in the required format and keep originals available. Never edit a transcript or create a replacement certificate yourself.

  • Passport
  • Final or provisional qualification
  • Transcript and grading scale
  • Accepted language evidence
  • Certified translation where required
  • Programme-specific CV, statement or portfolio
  • Recognition or authenticity evidence requested for enrolment

Universitaly pre-enrolment comes after the university route

Universitaly explains that applicants first choose a programme and contact or apply to the higher education institution. After the university process, eligible non-EU applicants living abroad complete online pre-enrolment and upload the records required for the institution's validation.

The validated pre-enrolment supports the visa application but does not create a right to a visa. Follow the competent embassy or consulate for the current appointment, financial, accommodation, insurance and document requirements.

A six-stage application tracker

Give each stage its own status and deadline so one completed form is not mistaken for the whole process.

  • 1. Programme and qualification fit
  • 2. University admission application
  • 3. Scholarship or fee-support application
  • 4. Admission decision and conditions
  • 5. Universitaly pre-enrolment where applicable
  • 6. Visa, travel and final enrolment

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Can I study in Italy without IELTS using an MOI letter?+

Only when the exact programme accepts prior English-medium education or that document as evidence. Read the programme call and obtain written confirmation where necessary.

What IELTS score is required for Italy?+

There is no national score for every degree. For example, Polimi currently lists IELTS 6 for its master's English requirement, while other universities and programmes publish their own B1, B2 or score rules.

Do I apply on Universitaly before applying to a university?+

Normally you first follow the university admission route. Universitaly pre-enrolment is a later step for relevant non-EU applicants living abroad, subject to the institution's instructions.

Does university admission guarantee the visa?+

No. The competent Italian diplomatic mission makes the visa decision and can require additional financial, accommodation, insurance and other evidence.

Can Pakistani qualifications be used for Italian admission?+

They can be assessed when they meet the selected degree's level, schooling, subject and documentation rules. The university decides academic eligibility and may request recognition or authenticity evidence.
