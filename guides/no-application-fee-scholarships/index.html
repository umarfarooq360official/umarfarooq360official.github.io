How to find scholarships with no application fee

Many major scholarship applications do not charge a scholarship form fee, but a connected university, test, document, visa or deposit cost may still apply. This guide shows how to verify every payment before you apply.

Updated 13 Sept 2026 Global English Editorial guide
Clear next stepsOfficial sources first
Use this guide with the official instructions.

Programme prompts, limits and document rules always take priority over general advice.

Separate the scholarship fee from the university fee

A scholarship portal can be free while the university admission route charges a separate application fee. The opposite can also happen when scholarship consideration is automatic with admission. Map every required portal and write the owner and payment beside it.

Also separate application charges from normal preparation costs: language tests, transcripts, certification, translations, passports, visa fees, medical checks, deposits and travel may occur even when scholarship submission is free.

Use official programmes as your starting point

Government and consortium programmes such as Chevening, Commonwealth, Erasmus Mundus and DAAD publish their application routes openly. KAUST publishes its graduate admissions process and fellowship information. Check each current portal for fee wording and then check the connected university or course separately.

AspireCompass does not label an application permanently fee-free because fees and routes can change. The official page at the time of submission is the only reliable confirmation.

A four-step payment verification

Never begin with a payment link sent by private message. Reach the programme through its official domain, open the current application instructions, identify every required portal and search each official fee page. If the wording is unclear, ask the institution through a published contact channel.

  • Confirm the organisation and exact intake
  • List scholarship and admission portals separately
  • Check official fee and waiver pages
  • Keep written confirmation of any waiver

How fee waivers work

Some universities offer need-based, country-based, event-based or programme-specific waivers. A waiver code from an unofficial forum may be invalid or misused. Follow the institution's published request process and never claim financial circumstances that are untrue.

Build a zero-surprise application budget

For every programme, record the scholarship form fee, university fee, test costs, document costs, passport, visa, health requirements, deposit, travel and the timing of the first stipend. Mark each item covered, waived, refundable or self-funded.

Recognise a scholarship payment scam

Warning signs include guaranteed selection, urgency to pay a private person, requests for cryptocurrency or gift cards, a look-alike domain, interviews conducted only through informal chat and demands for passwords or one-time codes.

A legitimate fee should appear in the official process and use a traceable institutional payment system. Even then, confirm what the fee covers and whether it is refundable before paying.

No-fee strategy for applicants with limited budgets

Prioritise strong-fit programmes with confirmed affordable processes, request official waivers early and avoid unnecessary repeated tests. A smaller, verified shortlist is safer than applying to dozens of unclear opportunities because a social post calls them free.

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Are fully funded scholarships always free to apply for?+

No. The scholarship form may be free while university admission or preparation costs remain. Verify each required portal and cost separately.

Does Chevening charge an application fee?+

Use Chevening's official application instructions for the current answer and separately check whether your selected universities charge admission fees.

Can I ask a university for an application-fee waiver?+

Yes, when it publishes a waiver route or contact process. Apply honestly and obtain confirmation before assuming the fee is removed.

Is a paid scholarship application automatically a scam?+

Not automatically; a connected university or legitimate service may charge a disclosed fee. The warning is an unverified payment, especially one linked to a guarantee or private account.

Should I pay an agent to secure a scholarship?+

No agent can guarantee selection. Use official portals, retain control of your account and verify any optional service independently.

Does AspireCompass submit applications for students?+

No. AspireCompass is an independent research and guidance website. Apply only through the programme, university, government or employer portal linked as the official source.

Can a deadline or eligibility rule change after this page is updated?+

Yes. Programme owners can revise dates, benefits and rules. Reopen the official call before you act, check the intake year and save a copy of the instructions used for your application.
