Editorial standards

How AspireCompass researches, writes and corrects information

Our editorial policy is designed around one principle: a useful summary must make its source, uncertainty and limits visible. Last updated 13 September 2026.

Reviewed 13 Sep 2026 Global English
Clear next stepsOfficial sources first

Source hierarchy

Primary sources come first: the programme owner, government, university, foundation, employer or authorised application body. We use their current programme page, call, application instructions or careers listing wherever available.

A reputable secondary source may help locate or understand an opportunity, but it does not replace the owner for a deadline or mandatory eligibility rule. Social posts, copied lists and search snippets are leads, not final evidence.

What is checked in a listing

Editors review the programme identity, opportunity type, intended level, destination, funding description, deadline wording, application route and source domain. Complex details are summarised conservatively, and readers are directed to the owner for the complete call.

  • Correct programme and intake
  • Current application status
  • Eligibility audience and major restrictions
  • Funding description and material caveats
  • Deadline, time zone and nomination route
  • Working official source

Meaning of status labels

Open is used when a current official cycle is accepting applications. Opening soon requires an official future window or clear announcement. Rolling describes vacancies or review without one universal closing date. Check official site is used for variable, uncertain or not-yet-announced cycles. Closed means the named cycle has passed or the owner states that it is closed.

Status is a snapshot, not a live guarantee. Every card includes a checked date so readers can judge freshness and repeat the official check before acting.

Original guides and responsible assistance

Application guides add original structure, examples, checklists and decision frameworks rather than copying programme pages. Examples are fictional or general and are clearly presented as instruction, not text to submit.

Research and editing tools may assist organisation, comparison or language review, but published claims must be checked against sources and reviewed for accuracy, usefulness and safety. We do not invent applicant stories, outcomes or programme rules.

Corrections and material updates

Reports are compared with a current primary source. A material deadline, status or eligibility error is prioritised. Minor style changes may not receive a public note, while checked dates and page update dates help readers understand when substantive information was reviewed.

Editorial independence and advertising

Advertising and technical monetisation do not determine whether an opportunity is listed, its status or its editorial description. Advertisers do not receive favourable eligibility treatment. Paid or sponsored editorial material, if ever introduced, will be clearly labelled and will not be presented as independent verification.

What publication does not mean

Inclusion is not an endorsement or guarantee. AspireCompass cannot test every external portal, predict every applicant's eligibility or monitor every change instantly. Readers must use the official source, protect personal data and exercise independent judgement.

How to report a problem

Use the contact page and include the AspireCompass URL, the exact statement at issue, the current official source and a short explanation. Do not send private application documents. Good-faith corrections are welcome from readers and programme owners.

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Does AspireCompass use primary sources?+

Yes. Programme owners, governments, universities and employers are the preferred evidence for dates, eligibility and application procedures.

How often is every page checked?+

Pages are reviewed according to deadline urgency and material changes rather than a guaranteed fixed interval. The visible checked or updated date lets readers judge freshness.

Can an advertiser change a listing?+

No. Advertising does not control editorial status, eligibility summaries or conclusions. Any sponsored content would require clear labelling.

How can I report an expired deadline?+

Send the page URL, exact issue and current programme-owner source through the contact address. Do not include personal application documents.

Does AspireCompass copy official scholarship pages?+

No. It summarises essential facts conservatively and adds original comparison and application guidance while linking readers to the complete official call.
