Help us keep opportunity information useful

Report an expired listing, incorrect detail or broken official link. Programme owners can also suggest an opportunity for editorial review.

Reviewed 29 Aug 2026 Global English
Corrections & submissions

hello@aspirecompass.com

Include the AspireCompass URL and an official programme source. We do not accept applications or personal documents.

Open email

Before you send

  • Use an official government, university, employer or programme link.
  • Do not send passports, transcripts, bank records or passwords.
  • We cannot check personal eligibility or guarantee selection.

What to include

Send the listing title, the page URL, the specific correction and an official source that confirms the change. For new opportunities, include the programme owner's application page and current deadline.

  • Correction or submission type
  • AspireCompass page URL
  • Official programme URL
  • Exact detail to update
  • Your contact email

Editorial independence

Submission does not guarantee publication. We prioritise legitimate, clearly documented opportunities and may decline unclear, fee-based or unverifiable programmes.
